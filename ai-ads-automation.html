WHAT IT IS

What is AI ads automation?

AI ads automation means producing ad creative with a pipeline instead of one video at a time. Product data, offers and briefs feed an automated workflow. AI models write the script and hooks, generate voice and visuals, add captions and brand elements, and render every size you need — 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, 1:1 and 4:5 for feeds, 16:9 for YouTube.

The result: enough creative variations to test every product, audience and hook, at a cost per video a traditional studio can’t reach. Renext builds these pipelines on n8n, with OpenAI for scripting, ElevenLabs for voice, and FFmpeg or Remotion for rendering — and keeps people in the loop for quality and brand safety.

QUESTIONS

AI ads automation FAQ

What is AI ads automation?

A pipeline that produces advertising creative with little manual work per ad: product data and briefs go in, and AI tools write scripts, generate voice and visuals, add captions and render finished video ads in every format. We run it on n8n with a human quality check before anything is published.

How many AI ads can the pipeline produce?

Standard setups are sized for 50 or 100 videos a day. Larger volumes are designed after a technical pilot. Throughput depends on your catalog, formats and approval steps, and is agreed in writing.

Do people check the ads?

Yes. Automated checks catch technical errors, and our editors review batches for brand fit, claims and quality before export or publishing.

Can it run on our own AWS account?

Yes. Turnkey Deployment installs the n8n pipeline, rendering workers and integrations on your AWS account, with handover documentation and training.

What does AI ads automation cost?

Managed pipelines start at USD 4,900 per month for 50 videos a day plus setup. A one-time Pipeline Blueprint costs USD 1,750. Generation, API, cloud, ad spend and taxes are separate.
