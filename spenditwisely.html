04 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SpendItWisely free to use?
Yes, SpendItWisely is completely free for consumers. Our business model is based on merchant partnerships and promotional programs — users never pay to use the core app.
How does receipt scanning work?
Take a photo of any bill. Our OCR technology extracts merchant name, items, amounts and tax components, then categorises them automatically. Users review and confirm the extraction before it's saved.
What are SpendCoins?
SpendCoins are promotional rewards earned through mindful spending and receipt organization. They can be redeemed at partner kirana stores for discounts and perks. SpendCoins are promotional reward units — not cash, not cryptocurrency.
Is my financial data secure?
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell your personal financial data to third parties. SpendItWisely only uses your data to power your own spending reports and rewards.
When will the app launch?
SpendItWisely is currently in active development by the Renext Technologies engineering team. Join our early access list by contacting us at info@renexttechnologies.com or calling +91 9548492221.
