How SIWSS Works for You

Smart Invoice & Wallet Savings System (SIWSS) turns complex Indian taxation into effortless, automated financial clarity.

1. Instant AI Receipt Scan

Snap bills from local eateries (like your daily Sardarji ke momos for ₹150), pharmacy bills, grocery marts, or fuel stations. SIWSS detects merchant details, invoice date, and itemized lines in 1 second.

2. Automatic GST Split

No more manual math. SIWSS automatically calculates CGST, SGST, and IGST breakdowns (e.g. ₹14 CGST + ₹14 SGST), ensuring every rupee of input tax credit is recorded for your business or personal balance sheet.

3. Rewards & Downloadable Reports

Earn coins for every bill uploaded to redeem on partner deals. Generate a monthly GST expenditure statement with 1 click, ready for your Chartered Accountant or self-filing.
