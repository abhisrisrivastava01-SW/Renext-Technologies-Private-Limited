People direct. AI produces. People check.

    Brief call

    20 minutes on your product, buyer, offer and where the ads will run.

  2. 02

    Concepts & hooks

    Written scripts and 3–5 opening hooks, approved by you before production.

  3. 03

    Production & edit

    AI scenes, presenters and voice, cut and finished by our video editors.

  4. 04

    Variations

    Formats for each placement and alternate hooks so you can test what works.

04 / QUESTIONS

Before you ask.

Are the ads fully AI-generated?

No. AI handles parts of production such as presenters, scenes and voice. People write the concept, direct the look, edit the cut and check every frame before delivery.

What do you need from us to start?

Your product, the audience, the offer, any brand assets, and the platforms you advertise on. A 20-minute call is usually enough to scope a pilot.

Can you use a real person’s face or voice?

Only with written consent from that person. Otherwise we use licensed or fully synthetic presenters and voices.

Which formats do you deliver?

9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, 1:1 and 4:5 for feeds, and 16:9 for YouTube, with captions burned in or as separate files.
