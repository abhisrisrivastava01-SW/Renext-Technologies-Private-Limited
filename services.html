01 — Financial Operations
[SYS.01] Account Payable

Account Payable Automation Service

Eliminate manual data entry, overpayment errors, and billing fraud. Renext delivers end-to-end Straight-Through Processing (STP) for modern enterprise finance departments, achieving automated 3-Way Matching between Purchase Orders, Goods Receipt Notes (GRN), and vendor invoices with 99.8% precision.

1. AI OCR/ICR Ingestion

Multi-currency line items, vendor details, and dates parsed from PDFs and physical scans automatically.

2. 3-Way PO Matching

Automated cross-validation against PO and GRN with customisable tolerance thresholds.

3. GL Auto-Coding & ERP

Direct 2-way sync with SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite, Dynamics 365, Tally, and Zoho Books.

4. Fraud & Duplicate Guard

Algorithmic duplicate invoice detection and dynamic early-payment discounting models.
